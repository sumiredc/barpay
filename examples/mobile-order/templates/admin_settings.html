How the cover charge works

Rate per hour
The AI sets it within the range you set, in steps of $0.05. When you change the range, a rate outside it moves into it at once.
Charging
During a visit, each time the set minutes pass, the rate for those minutes is added. Minutes the customer leaves partway through are not added.
Free after an order
The first order doesn't count. After each later order, nothing is added for this long. An order during that time starts it again.
Cap per visit
Once the cover charge reaches this amount, nothing more is added in that visit. 0 for no cap.
Pricing goal
What the AI aims for when it sets the rate. Toward the left, it lowers the rate when the room is quiet, to bring people in. Toward the right, it raises the rate when the room is busy, to grow revenue. "When it's quiet" and "When it's busy" under the slider are a rough guide to the direction asked of the AI. The actual rate is set by the AI from how busy the room is at the time, and always stays within the range. With each decision, the AI writes how it served the goal in the log on the Now tab.
When changes apply
The range: as soon as you save. The pricing goal: from the next update. Every, free after an order and the cap per visit: for each customer in the store, from the next set of minutes that starts.
